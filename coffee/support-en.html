Support — Coffee Journal

Start a journal

Tap +, choose a coffee type and save your photos, place and impressions. Location, photos and ratings are optional. Signature drinks are supported.

Find and delete notes

Search starts hidden; pull down at the top of the journal to reveal it. Filter by time, city or favorites. Long-press a note and confirm Delete, or edit and delete from its detail page.

Maps and offline use

Notes can be saved offline. Search cafés worldwide or pin a location on the online map. If a place is missing, enter its name and city manually without location permission. Map coverage and search results depend on the provider.

Protect and restore your journal

Export a full backup in Settings and keep it somewhere safe. Each JSON file must be under 300 MB. Import merges records and keeps the newer version of each duplicate. Backups are not encrypted. Back up before uninstalling; there is no in-app iCloud sync, and the developer cannot recover unbacked-up entries.

Report a problem

Email your app version, OS version, device model and steps to reproduce the issue. You may include a screenshot with personal details removed; avoid sending unnecessary photos or complete backups. Email: wujianrenn@gmail.com.
