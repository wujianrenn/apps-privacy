Privacy policy — Coffee Journal

2026-09-23

Your coffee journal

Coffee Journal (啡迹) is a free personal coffee journal. No account is required. The app has no advertising, in-app purchases, subscriptions, tracking SDKs or developer-operated data servers. This version does not provide AI reviews or cross-device iCloud sync.

Information stored on your device

Notes, dates and time zones, coffee types, ratings, impressions, tags, favorites, café names, addresses, cities, countries or regions, map coordinates and selected photos are stored in local app files. Appearance and language preferences are stored in system preferences. The developer cannot access this content through the app.

Maps and location

Maps, place searches and address lookups use Apple MapKit and Core Location. Requests may send search text, map regions or coordinates to Apple and its map service providers. The app reads your current location to help choose a café only after you use the location feature and grant permission; it does not continuously track you in the background. You can enter places manually without location access. Map coverage and results vary by region and network. These services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Photos and camera

The system photo picker provides only the images you select. Taking a photo requires camera permission. Added photos are resized and re-encoded as JPEG without the original EXIF metadata; originals in your library are unchanged. The app does not automatically upload photos. You can revoke camera and location permissions in system Settings.

Backup, sharing and deletion

Exported JSON backups contain notes and photos and are not encrypted. If you choose a cloud drive or share with someone, the chosen service or recipient handles that content. App data may also be included in system device backups to iCloud or a computer; this is separate from in-app cross-device sync. Deleting a note removes its local record and photos no other note uses. Uninstalling removes local app data. Exported files, copies received by others and system backups are not automatically deleted with a note and must be managed separately.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content you provide, use them only to respond and resolve your request, and delete them when no longer needed. Avoid sending unnecessary private photos, locations or full backups. Contact wujianrenn@gmail.com for requests about personal information in support correspondence.

Policy updates

Changes to this policy will be published at the same address with an updated date. New online features will be reflected in the policy. Contact: wujianrenn@gmail.com.
